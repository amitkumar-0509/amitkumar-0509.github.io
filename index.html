Who I Am

Hi! I'm Amit Kumar, an analytical and detail-oriented Data Analyst based in Chandigarh, India. I hold an M.Sc. in Mathematics from Himachal Pradesh University, Shimla — which gave me a deep foundation in statistics, probability, and data modelling.
